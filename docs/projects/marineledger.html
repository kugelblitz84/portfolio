01 / Overview

The product and my part in it.

A private shipping ledger and accounts app where I handled client communication, requirement analysis, UI/UX planning, Flutter implementation, and delivery support.

My role

Acted as the representative developer from ShafaCode, communicated with the client, understood their business model, suggested features and usage flows, designed the UI/UX, and built the Flutter app.

02 / The challenge

What needed to change.

The client needed a private tool for shipping ledger management, account calculation, operational records, and practical daily usage. The budget did not include a dedicated backend.

03 / My contribution

From decisions to implementation.

  • Communicated directly with the client to understand shipping workflows, accounting model, and operational constraints.
  • Designed and implemented the Flutter UI/UX and core app flow from scratch.
  • Built ledger/accounting flows, operational records, transaction/calculation logic, and business-specific data handling.
  • Implemented local-first functionality and safeguards appropriate for a private isolated business environment.
  • Provided consultation on feature usage and practical improvements based on the client’s working process.

04 / The outcome

From requirements to delivery

Delivered Flutter ledger and accounting flows with local-first functionality, supported by direct client consultation and delivery support.
